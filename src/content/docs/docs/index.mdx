Surface Labs is a procedural texture and material authoring application.

It's built as an independent, portable alternative to tools like Substance
Designer and Material Maker, with no subscription model and no cloud dependency.
The application runs fully offline. The only thing you give up is the in-app
documentation. Projects are ordinary files on disk, so you can back them up or
move them between machines with Git, Dropbox, or a copy and paste.

## Who it's for

Everyone. Surface Labs came out of needing non-subscription software as an indie
developer at a small studio with no funding.

It's new to the market, and it's ready for professional pipelines: Unity, Unreal
Engine, Godot, VFX, or anything else that takes PBR textures.

## What makes it different

### It's f****** fast

A Rust engine delivers up to 400x faster per-node render times on comparable
graphs. Most individual nodes render in under a millisecond.

### It works everywhere

Native builds for every major operating system. Start a sketch on your tablet
and polish it on your desktop.

### MCP is built in

The MCP server talks natively to the engine, so agentic workflows can drive your
pipeline from project creation through to export.

### No subscriptions

A 3D project runs for years, and a subscription texturing tool bills you for
every month of it. Surface Labs is a one-time purchase. Over the life of a
project that's hundreds of dollars saved, for comparable capability.

## How it works

Like most procedural workflows, textures are noise layered together into
patterns and recognizable materials. The graph is non-destructive and puts
tiling first, so you can iterate and polish all you'd like.
