Surface Labs is a procedural texture and material authoring application.

It's built as an independent, portable alternative to tools like Substance
Designer and Material Maker, with no subscription model and no cloud dependency.
The application runs fully offline. Projects are ordinary files on disk, so you can back them up or
move them between machines with Git, Dropbox, or a copy and paste.

<small>*the in-app manual downloads from the website the first time the app is
online, then reads from disk and works offline</small>

## Who it's for

Everyone! Surface Labs came out of needing non-subscription software as an indie developer
with a limited budget.

It's new to the market, and it has the means to fill in professional pipelines
working in Unity, Unreal Engine, Godot, VFX, or anything else that takes PBR
textures.

## What makes it different

#### It's f****** fast

A Rust engine delivers multiple times faster performance on many nodes of comparable features.
Most Noises render in less than 1ms on Desktop and Tablets, with many nodes running compute shaders
for large performance gains.

The [Surface Labs Speed Test](/blog/surface-labs-speed-test/) measures this node
by node against Substance 3D Designer 13.1.2, including the one bracket where
Substance comes out ahead.

#### It works everywhere

Native builds for most major operating systems. Start a sketch on your tablet
and polish it on your desktop.

#### MCP is built in

The MCP server talks natively to the engine, so agentic workflows can drive your
pipeline from project creation through to export.

#### No subscriptions

A 3D project runs for years, and a subscription texturing tool bills you for
every month of it. Surface Labs is a one-time purchase: hundreds of dollars
saved over the life of a project, for comparable capability.

## How it works

Like most procedural workflows, textures are noise layered together into
patterns and recognizable materials. The graph is non-destructive and puts
tiling first, so you can iterate and polish all you'd like.
