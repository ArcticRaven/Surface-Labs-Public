## 1.2.2

The 3D preview

- Rebuilt around six option sections (Model, Maps, Surface, Lighting,
  Environment, Display), each with its own Reset and a dot marking changes.
- A corner toolbar over the render itself for camera, turntable, exposure and
  the tonemapper. One click pops the preview out to fill the window.
- Inspect any single map on the model, unlit and unprocessed. Cycle the maps a
  material actually has, and pick which PBR Output the preview shows.
- The preview only redraws when something changes, and exports run off the
  render thread. On weak graphics hardware it steps down instead of failing.
- Windows gained a Vulkan option next to DirectX 12.

Materials

- Thickness adds subsurface scattering, replacing the old preview-only slider.
- Emissive lets parts of a material glow, in the preview and on export.
- Bloom lets bright highlights and emissives spill past their edges. Off by
  default.
- Height maps cast their own shadows under the key light. A new Parallax mode
  adds fine detail without subdividing, and the Depth slider drives both height
  modes.
- A new AgX tonemapper alongside ACES.

Preview and export now agree

- The preview-only shading sliders are gone. The renderer shades with the
  values from the PBR Output node.
- The Opacity input works, reflections are sharper, and the mip chain is
  filtered in linear light so colored textures stop darkening as they recede.

Node library

- Voronoi and Cells cut their walls from the true distance to the region
  boundary, with Edge Width, Edge Softness and Rounding.
- Brick's Hexagon lattice lays an actual honeycomb, and Herringbone is a real
  zigzag weave.
- Quick Blur no longer ghosts past small radii. Big blurs and grows lost their
  dropout speckle, onion rings and cut-out halos.
- Slope Blur has a gradual Intensity response and no longer speckles or
  terraces.
- Perlin, fBm, Clouds and Gabor span their full range.
- Region Query gained its own Seed and a Random Slope mode.
- Height Blend's mask is a real second output next to the height. Projects that
  had the switch set to Mask need to rewire from the new port.
- Levels gained a Channel selector. Edge Detect gained Width, Rounding and
  Smoothing. Bricks gained Corner Radius, and Tile Sampler a Rounding option.

Two of these change how existing projects look: mid-range Slope Blur values
read a little softer, and the widened noise ranges read a touch more contrasty.
A Levels node pulls the old range back where you need it.

The manual

- No longer bundled. It downloads on first launch and stays cached for offline
  use, so it is always current and the install is smaller.
- Reachable from the toolbar search field, which finds manual articles and
  nodes in the same list.

Also in 1.2.1

- The toolbar became one row of icons around a single search field.
- Import your own .obj or .glb into the 3D preview. It embeds into the project,
  and a new Model UV node bakes the model's surface into texture data.
- Full trim sheet support: preview and export a whole sheet as one material,
  including from the command line.
- A new Material node reuses a whole material inside another one.
