This announcement was written before the day it appeared. Posts carry the moment
they go live, and the app reveals each one when that moment arrives — so a
month of them can be prepared in a single sitting and still arrive on schedule.

The timing is an absolute instant rather than a calendar date, which means
everyone sees a post appear at the same moment regardless of where they are. The
date printed above the card is the one the announcement was written for, so it
reads the same in Tokyo as it does in Vancouver.

If the app happens to be open when a scheduled post becomes due, it appears on
its own. Nothing needs restarting.
