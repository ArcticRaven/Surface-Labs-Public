title: "Some notices remove themselves"
date: "2026-08-01 09:00"
expires: "2026-08-05 00:00"
kind: notice
summary: "An announcement can carry an end date and disappear on its own when it passes."
---

Not everything worth announcing is worth keeping. A maintenance window, a
time-limited offer, a warning about a bug that has since been fixed — all of
them stop being useful the moment they stop being true.

An announcement can carry an end date. Once it passes, the card disappears
without anything needing to be republished, and without you having to dismiss
it.

The end date is checked every time the list is drawn rather than only when the
announcement arrives, so a notice still expires on time on a machine that has
been offline since before the deadline.
