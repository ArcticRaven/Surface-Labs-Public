Until now, the only way to hear that something had changed was to check Discord
or notice a new version on the store page. Announcements close that gap: this
card is written on the website and appears here on its own.

## What you will see here

- **Releases**, when a new build is available, with a link to get it.
- **Devlogs**, when there is something worth reading about how a feature works.
- **Notices**, for anything time-sensitive — a documentation outage, a change to
  how something behaves.

Posts are dated newest first. Anything you have not read yet carries a small dot
in its corner, which clears once the card has been on screen for a moment.

## It works offline

The last announcements you received stay on screen whether or not you have a
connection, and the release notes on the Changes tab ship with the app itself.
If a check fails, nothing breaks — the card simply shows what it already had.

Announcements can be switched off entirely in Settings, under General, along
with a control to clear anything already downloaded.

![The Surface Labs editor with every panel open](../../assets/docs/editor-all-panels.webp)

Nothing here is tied to the version you are running. Every announcement reaches
every build, so an older install sees exactly what a current one does.
